# A Systematic Analysis of Chunking Strategies for Reliable Question Answering

I read Sofia Bennani and Charles Moslonka’s `main.tex` from the supplied arXiv v1 source archive. The source is readable without a TeX installation. [arXiv 2601.14123](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14123)

The study used English Wikipedia and the Natural Questions short-answer subset. SPLADE ranked passages, Ministral 8B Instruct answered, and the generator tokenizer enforced a context budget. The reported grid varied chunk method, size (50–500), overlap (0% or 20% in the methods), and evidence context (500 to 10,000 tokens). The authors measured BERTScore, exact match, and abstention. They held reranking out of the experiment. [Experimental setup](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.14123v1)

In that setup, sentence chunks matched semantic chunks through roughly 5,000 evidence tokens. Overlap increased the index size without an improvement the authors could measure. For the illustrated sentence setting, semantic quality fell after roughly 2,500 tokens and exact match peaked near that budget. Abstention also changed with context size. The paper’s suggested 150–300-token chunks and zero overlap give Apollo a candidate experiment. Its numeric results come from English short-answer QA with a different retriever and generator.
